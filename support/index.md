# Support

Contact: [support@builtsafely.com](mailto:support@builtsafely.com)

## Common questions

**Which devices can scan?** iPhones and iPads with a LiDAR scanner (Pro models). Other devices
running iOS or iPadOS 27 can open, edit and export scans made on a LiDAR device with the same
Apple Account, and can try the demo scan.

**Where are my files?** In the Files app under iCloud Drive › Plan & Model › Exports, and in
Finder on your Mac under iCloud Drive › Plan & Model. If iCloud Drive is off, they are in
Files › On My iPhone › Plan & Model.

**How accurate is it?** Accuracy depends on the room and the scan. For important dimensions,
measure one wall with a tape or laser and enter it in the plan's calibration; every length is
then scaled to match.

**How do I open the files in Blender or FreeCAD?** Use the Blender or FreeCAD destination in a
scan's Files tab; the guides cover import settings.

**How do I use a scan with Claude?** Share the Claude bundle from a scan's Files tab, or install
the [Mac helper](../mcp/) so Claude can read your scans directly.

**I stopped subscribing. What happens to my files?** Nothing: every file stays. Pro files are not
updated after later edits until you subscribe again.

**Restore a purchase:** Settings › Restore Purchases.
