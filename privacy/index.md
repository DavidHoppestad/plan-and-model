# Privacy policy

Effective date: 13 November 2026.

**Plan & Model does not collect any data.** The app has no account and no server of its own, and
it contains no analytics or advertising code.

## Your scans

- Scans and the files made from them are saved in your own iCloud Drive (in the folder
  "Plan & Model"), or only on your device if iCloud Drive is off. Apple stores and syncs iCloud
  Drive under your Apple Account; we cannot see it.
- A scan contains the measured shape of the room, the names you give rooms, and the device model
  and iOS version that made it. Future versions that add photo textures will store camera images
  of the room in the same place; you will be able to turn their syncing off.
- The app never reads your location.

## Camera

The camera and LiDAR scanner measure the room while you scan. Video is processed on your device
and is not saved or sent anywhere.

## Purchases

Subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. We receive no payment details. The app
keeps a record of whether you are subscribed on your device so it works offline.

## Diagnostics

If you agree to share analytics with Apple, Apple may share crash and performance reports with us
through App Store Connect. They do not identify you.

## The Mac helper

The optional lidarscan-mcp helper runs only on your Mac. It reads your scans from iCloud Drive so
Claude can answer questions about them, and never changes or sends your files anywhere itself.
What you then share with Claude is covered by Anthropic's privacy policy.
