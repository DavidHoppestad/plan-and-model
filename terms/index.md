## Subscriptions

Plan & Model Pro is an auto-renewing subscription, monthly or yearly. The yearly plan starts with
a 7-day free trial. Payment is charged to your Apple Account at confirmation of purchase. The
subscription renews automatically unless you turn it off at least 24 hours before the end of the
current period; manage or cancel it in Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Files you made while
subscribed remain yours after a subscription ends.

## Measurements

Measurements come from your device's LiDAR scanner and are estimates. Check critical dimensions
with a tape or laser measure before building or ordering.
